Heart Transplant Eating List

A personal food reference app for heart transplant recipients.

Heart Transplant Eating List helps you quickly check whether a food is safe to choose, should be limited, or should be avoided after a heart transplant — plus a barcode scanner to check packaged foods against your own sodium target.

Food Guide

Browse and search a categorized list of foods with Choose / Limit / Avoid guidance and short explanations.

Barcode Scanner

Scan a packaged food's barcode to check its sodium content against the daily limit you set.

My Targets

Enter the numbers your transplant team gave you — stored privately, only on your device.

Not medical advice This app is a personal reference tool, not a substitute for guidance from your transplant dietitian or care team. Always confirm your diet plan, daily limits, and any medication interactions with them directly.
