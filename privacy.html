Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Chassis & Co LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Heart Transplant Eating List mobile app (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary: The App does not create an account for you, does not collect or transmit your personal or health information, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. Nearly everything the App does happens entirely on your device.

What stays on your device

Your personal dietary targets (e.g. daily sodium limit) that you enter in "My Targets" are saved locally on your device only.

Your history of previously scanned food products is saved locally on your device only, so you can look items up again without a signal.

Your Daily Log (weight, temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, blood glucose, and comments) is saved locally on your device only. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it yourself, for example by tapping Share to send it to your care team.

None of the above is ever sent to us, synced to any account, or shared with any third party. We have no way to see it.

What is sent over the network

When you scan a barcode, the App sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, independent, public food-product database, to retrieve the product's name, brand, and nutrition information. No personal information, device identifier, or account information is included in this request. This lookup is governed by Open Food Facts' own privacy practices, available at world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Subscriptions and payments

The App is offered as an auto-renewing yearly subscription with a free trial. All purchases, trials, renewals, and cancellations are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. We never receive your name, payment card, or billing details. Apple provides us only with anonymous, aggregated sales reports.

Camera access

The App uses your device's camera only to detect a barcode in real time while the scan screen is open. The App does not take, save, or store photos or video.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Since your targets and scan history are stored only on your device, you can delete them at any time by deleting the App, which removes all locally stored data along with it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@chassisandco.com.

Medical disclaimer The App is a personal reference tool and is not medical advice. It is not a substitute for guidance from your transplant dietitian or care team. Always confirm your diet plan, daily limits, and any medication interactions directly with them.
