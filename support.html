Frequently asked questions

How does the subscription work?

The app includes a free 1-week trial, then renews at $9.99 per year (or the equivalent in your local currency) until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the trial ends.

How do I cancel or restore my subscription?

To cancel, open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or tap Manage Subscription in the app's My Targets tab. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or renewal date to avoid being charged. On a new or reinstalled iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

Why is the camera showing a permission request?

The Scan tab uses your camera only to read barcodes in real time. No photos or video are ever saved. You can review or change camera access anytime in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

Does scanning a barcode require an internet connection?

Yes, the first time you scan a specific product — the app looks up its nutrition info from Open Food Facts, a public food database. After that, the result is saved on your device, so you can look up that same product again without a signal.

Is my dietary target information private?

Yes. The sodium limits and other targets you enter are stored only on your device. We never see them, and they're never uploaded anywhere.

Is this app a substitute for advice from my transplant team?

No. This app is a personal reference tool only. Always follow the diet plan, limits, and guidance given to you directly by your transplant dietitian or care team.

How do I share my Daily Log with my care team?

Open the Daily Log tab and tap the Share button in the top corner to send your entries by Messages, Mail, or any app you choose. Your log is never uploaded anywhere unless you share it.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app from your device removes all locally stored data, including your targets, scan history, and Daily Log. To remove a single Daily Log entry, swipe left on it.
